Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 30, 2026

1. Data Minimization

We only process and retain business-relevant professional contact data and technical mail server responses. We do not store email message bodies, passwords in plain text, or full crawler HTML pages beyond short-lived analysis.

2. User Retention & Account Deletion

Users can configure data retention periods (from 7 to 365 days) in their account settings. Account deletion immediately cascades and permanently purges all verifications, jobs, API keys, and stored data.

3. Right to Opt-Out / Suppression

Any individual or organization may request immediate exclusion of their email or domain from discovery and verification via our public Opt-Out form.
